Our Philosophy

A single point of intention,
expanding outward

Like ripples from a singing bowl, every session at Intentions begins at a centre — your intention — and moves outward through sound, energy, symbol and conversation.

We do not tie our practice to any single tradition. Instead, we hold a refined, grounded space where intuition is heard, balance is renewed, and personal development continues long after you leave the room.

  • Focus — clarifying what truly matters to you
  • Intuition — honouring the wisdom you already carry
  • Resonance — restoring balance through sound and stillness
  • Growth — movement that continues beyond the session

Kai Fang

Spiritual Practitioner

Kai Fang has been reading for others for four years. Through tarot and Shuyi, he helps clients understand their strengths, patterns and the path ahead with warmth, directness and honesty.

Offerings

  • Tarot + Shuyi Readings
  • Outlook for the year (by year/month/day)
  • Bi-monthly Tarot Preview
  • Bi-monthly Shuyi Preview
  • Tarot workshops(Basic, Intermediate and Advanced Levels)
Read Kai Fang's full profile

Since he was eight years old, Kai Fang has had dreams that come true. The most vivid have been of funerals. Every one he has attended, he had already dreamt, and each played out exactly as it had in the dream, moment for moment.

Growing up, he wanted to understand why he had this gift and what he was meant to do with it. That search for answers led him to tarot, and later to Shuyi (数易), a Chinese numerology system. For the past four years he has been reading for others.

Whichever practice he uses, Kai Fang's readings follow the same path. They begin with you: your natural strengths, your shortcomings, and the patterns that shape how you move through life. From that understanding, he looks ahead to the situations and turning points likely to come your way. Together, you then work out the best path forward for the year ahead. That means living in line with where your life is naturally inclined to go, so you face less resistance, working around what cannot be changed, and working on what can.

Tarot and Shuyi each bring something different to that process. Tarot draws on the Jungian tradition, treating the cards as a mirror of the psyche that brings the present moment and your inner life into focus. Shuyi reads the deeper structure of your life through its numbers and the Five Elements, showing how the energies within you support or strain one another.

Kai Fang is known for being warm, direct and honest. He will not tell you what you want to hear. His aim is simple: that you leave a session knowing yourself better, seeing what lies ahead more clearly, and with a clear sense of how to meet it.

James Chong

Wellness Practitioner

James works with images, symbols and sound — the parts of our experience that words cannot reach. At Intentions he uses OH Cards to open conversations that may be difficult to begin directly, alongside career coaching and individual sound healing with a Nepalese Singing Moon Bowl.

Offerings

  • Individual OH Cards Exploration with Oracle Cards
  • Career Coaching
  • Individual Singing Moon Bowl sessions
  • OH Cards Workshops
Read James's full profile

James brings together personal reflection, spiritual exploration and sound through OH Cards, oracle cards, career coaching and individual Singing Moon Bowl sessions. His approach invites people to explore their experiences, recognise what matters to them and consider their next step at their own pace.

James is interested in how people come to understand themselves: the patterns they recognise, the choices they face, and the experiences they find difficult to put into words. His approach brings together personal reflection, spiritual exploration and practical conversations about life direction.

At Intentions, James uses OH Cards to invite exploration through images, words and personal associations. A picture may bring a feeling, memory or question into focus, creating a starting point for conversation. He may also incorporate oracle cards for those who wish to explore personal meaning through a spiritual lens. Each person’s interpretation and experience guide the conversation.

James also offers career coaching for people considering a change, navigating uncertainty or seeking a stronger connection between their work and what matters to them. These conversations explore strengths, values and possibilities, helping individuals consider their next steps with greater awareness.

His individual sound healing sessions use a Nepalese Singing Moon Bowl, offering time to pause, listen and attend to bodily sensations. Sessions may include a focus on chakras, with the approach discussed according to the individual’s interests and comfort.

Across his work, James values curiosity, intuition and personal choice. Whether someone begins with a question, a life transition or an interest in exploring their inner world, he offers a thoughtful space to reflect at their own pace and consider what feels meaningful to them.
